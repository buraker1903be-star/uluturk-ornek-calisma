Türkiye cuts nuclear construction withholding to 1%. Who actually keeps the difference?

By [Partner name] Published 20 May 2026 Updated 3 June 2026 7 min
THE SHORT ANSWER

The rate fell from 5% to 1% for progress payments made after 1 June 2026. Whether the saving reaches the contractor or stays with the employer turns on one thing: whether the contract price was agreed inclusive or exclusive of Turkish taxes.

Presidential Decision no. 11344, published in the Official Gazette on 19 May 2026, is short and unambiguous. The difficulty is not the rate. It is that most EPC contracts signed before it were priced on the old one.

What the tax clause decides

Long-term construction contracts price Turkish withholding in one of three ways, and each produces a different outcome now:

  • Tax-inclusive fixed priceThe reduced withholding improves the contractor's cash position directly.
  • Tax-exclusive priceThe saving stays with the employer, and the contractor has limited grounds to claim it.
  • Price with a change-in-law clauseThe outcome depends on whether the clause runs one way, for increases only, or symmetrically.

Three things worth checking now

  • Straddling paymentsWhich rate applied to progress payments certified before 1 June but paid after it.
  • Subcontract alignmentWhether subcontracts carry the same tax clause as the head contract.
  • Financial modelWhether the cash-flow model still assumes 5%, and what that does to covenant headroom.
Basis: Presidential Decision no. 11344, Official Gazette no. 33258 of 19 May 2026 · Income Tax Law no. 193, arts. 42 and 94 · Corporate Tax Law no. 5520, arts. 15 and 30.

Questions we are asked

FOR FOREIGN COUNSEL
Can an LCIA award be enforced against assets in Türkiye?

Yes. Türkiye has been a party to the New York Convention since 1992, subject to the reciprocity and commercial reservations. Enforcement is sought before the Turkish commercial court at the debtor's domicile, and interim attachment may be requested to preserve assets ahead of the hearing.

How quickly can a vessel be arrested in a Turkish port?

An arrest application based on a maritime claim under the Turkish Commercial Code can be heard within days, and often the same day where the claim is well documented. Counter-security is normally required from the applicant.

Is ISTAC a realistic alternative to ICC for Türkiye-connected contracts?

For disputes with a strong Turkish nexus, yes. ISTAC's fee scale is materially lower, proceedings may be conducted in Turkish or English, and the seat is domestic, which simplifies enforcement. ICC remains preferable where an award will be enforced across several jurisdictions.

Does a foreign law firm need Turkish co-counsel to appear?

Rights of audience before Turkish courts are reserved to lawyers admitted to a Turkish bar. Foreign firms therefore instruct local counsel for litigation and enforcement, while retaining conduct of the wider international matter.
